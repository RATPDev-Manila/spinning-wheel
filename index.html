Privacy information

This app records the name, work email, organization, prize won, time of the spin, and optional marketing choice. Spin records are stored in this kiosk browser until an organizer downloads them as a CSV. Anyone with access to the device or downloaded file may be able to read them.

The event organizer must define how long browser records and downloaded copies are kept, and who is authorized to access them.

The form data is stored in this kiosk browser; it is not sent to an event server by this page.

Before collecting attendee details, the event organizer must confirm the responsible RATP Dev entity, legal basis and purpose for each use, who will access the browser and CSV file, retention period, and applicable hosting/CDN arrangements. These details are not yet established in this app; this notice is not a confirmation of compliance.

For questions or to exercise data-protection rights, contact dpo@ratpdev.com. Confirm with the event organizer that this is the applicable DPO contact for this event.
